TITLE (sentence case, no abbreviations):
Order and entropy of lattices of unit vectors in d dimensions: label-free discovery of unusual local organization in simulated lattices and synthetic images

ABSTRACT (188 words, limit 200):
Averaged order parameters say how ordered a lattice of orientational states is, not where its organization is unusual. We extend the local order and entropy quantities of the MyUncle maintenance-lattice model to unit vectors in d dimensions (von Mises-Fisher distribution); they equal MyUncle's at d=2. On this basis we build a label-free score that judges how rare the state of a site is at several prevalence scales and smooths it over the lattice, and compare it with four baselines, with settings frozen before the held-out data. On lattices simulated with MyUncle, smoothing lowered the average precision of every baseline because the damaged sites are not compact regions. On synthetic stained-tissue images, smoothing raised spatial coherence for every scorer, and the frozen score was the best method with DINOv2 embeddings and a poor one with handcrafted features. A supervised search over 360 configurations with GaaR, an evolutionary search framework, raised the lower-tail average precision on fresh test scenarios from 0.18 to 0.26; random search also beat the default, so the gain belongs to the selected configuration, not to the search. All evidence is simulated; code and results are open.

KEYWORDS:
lattice models; von Mises-Fisher distribution; orientational order; entropy; unsupervised anomaly detection; spatial smoothing

HIGHLIGHTS (3-5, <=85 characters each):
- MyUncle order and entropy extend to unit vectors in d dimensions
- Label-free score: state rarity at several scales, smoothed over the lattice
- Settings frozen on development data; held-out data evaluated once
- Smoothing helps when anomalies form regions and hurts when they do not
- A GaaR configuration search helped on fresh tests; the search was not decisive
